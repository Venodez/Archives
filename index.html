How it works

What every number on the site means, and how the ranking is worked out.

ClassificaRanking

Points
Earned in tournaments only. Each tournament gives points for how far you went, and they add up over the season.
+
The points a player earned in their last tournament.
Wins – Losses
Tournament matches only.
Ties
Equal points: more titles first, then the best finish.
2v2
In a team tournament, both players earn the points of their team's finish.
