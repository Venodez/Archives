ClassificaRanking

ELO
Your score. Everyone starts a season at 1000. Win and you gain points, lose and you drop. Beating someone ranked above you is worth more, losing to someone below you costs more. An even match moves about 16 points.
▲ / ▼
How many points the player won or lost in their last match.
Wins – Losses
Every ranked and tournament match counts.
Win rate
The share of matches won.
Last 5
The last five results: a gold dot is a win, an empty dot a loss.
Streak
Shown when someone wins 3 matches or more in a row.
