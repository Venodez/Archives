相場Value list

Value
What a skin is worth in trades, set by the community. Use it to compare skins with each other, not as a price set by the game.
?
The skin has no value yet, usually because it's new or too rare to have enough trades. Skins at ? can't be scored in the calculator.
Demand
How much people want the skin, from 1 to 10. High demand means it's easy to trade away. Low demand means you may have to wait or add a bit to find a trade.
Base weapon
The weapon the skin goes on, like Redliner, Monarch or Phoenix. Some skins exist on two weapons, for example Sub-Zero.
Collection
The set the skin comes from, like Precede All or Dragonhunt.
Worth in AWPs
The skin's value counted in AWPs (one AWP = 150). A quick way to picture how big a value is.

動向Trends

—Stable
The value isn't moving. Safe to trade at the listed value.
▲Rising
The value is going up. People are paying more for it than before.
▼Lowering
The value is going down, or fewer people want it right now.
≈Unstable
Trades for it are all over the place, so the real value is hard to pin down. Be careful.
✶Hyped
Lots of people want it right now, often because it's new. Hype can fade fast.

取引Calculator

You give / You get
Put your skins on the left and the other person's skins on the right. The site adds up both sides.
W
A win: you get more than 5% more value than you give.
L
A loss: you give more than 5% more value than you get.
Fair
Both sides are within 5% of each other.
Copy image
Makes a picture of your trade that you can paste straight into Discord.

所持品Inventory

Total listed value
All your skins added up at their listed value. Skins at ? aren't counted.
Copy as text
Copies a ready-made Discord message listing your skins, with a coloured square for each rarity.
Your device only
Your inventory and calculator are saved in your browser. Nobody else can see them, and they're gone if you clear your browser data.

掲示板Trade board

Has / Wants
What the person is offering, and what they want in return.
Open to offers
They'll look at other offers too, not only what they listed.
Good deal
They ask for less value than they offer: good for you.
Fair
What they ask and what they offer are close in value.
Asks more
They ask for more value than they offer.
48 hours
Posts delete themselves after 48 hours. You can delete your own post anytime, and report posts that break the rules.

用語Trading words

Overpay
Giving more than the listed value to get a skin you really want. If a skin often gets overpays, its value tends to rise.
Trade down
Trading one expensive skin for several cheaper ones.
Trade up
Trading several cheaper skins for one more expensive skin.
Cross-trading
Trading Redliner skins for things outside the game, like items from another game or real money. Not allowed here.
